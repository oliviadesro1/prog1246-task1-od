What you need to know before adopting a guinea pig

So, you want to adopt a guinea pig! Here are a few things you need to consider before taking home your furry friends.

Housing

For one guinea pig, your enclosure should be at least 7 square feet (about 42 x 24 inches, or 106 x 61 cm). For each additional guinea pig, add 2-4 square feet. The cage should have a solid bottom to prevent foot injuries. The cage should be lined with an absorbant fleece liner and/or disposable bedding such as shredded newspaper or aspen shavings. Guinea pigs are social animals and are happiest in groups, so consider adopting two or more!

Nutrition

A well-rounded guinea pig diet consists of an unlimited supply of hay timothy or orchard hay, 1/8 cup of guinea pig pellets, one cup of fresh vegetables, and a small portion of fruit a few times a week. Some common vegetables that are safe for daily feeding include:

  • Green leaf, red leaf, romaine, and butter lettuce
  • Bell peppers
  • Cilantro
  • Cucumber

Some vegetables (e.g., broccoli, carrots, cabbage) are high in certain nutrients and can cause gas and bloating. Check the nutritional content of a new vegetable before feeding.
